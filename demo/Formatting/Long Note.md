# Long note

What to look for: this note is a few hundred lines long on purpose, to
exercise viewport-only layout and scrolling - it should scroll smoothly
with no visible lag, and the readable-line-length setting (Settings >
Editor) should cap how wide a paragraph gets even though the window is
wider than that. The content itself is a short, honest history of
plain-text note-taking, section by section, so scrolling through it reads
as something rather than filler.

## 1. Paper index cards

Before software, researchers kept one fact per card and filed them by subject, an approach Niklas Luhmann scaled into tens of thousands of cross-referenced notes. That habit shaped what later software chose to make easy. The constraint mattered more than the tool: what could not be done cheaply was rarely attempted.

## 2. The outline processor

The constraint mattered more than the tool: what could not be done cheaply was rarely attempted. Every generation of note-taking software re-discovers roughly the same handful of ideas.
