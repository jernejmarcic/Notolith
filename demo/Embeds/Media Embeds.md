# Audio and video embeds

What to look for: a player card with play/pause, a seek bar and a clock.
Both files' source and licence are in `demo-assets.md`. Media playback is
optional at build time (`NOTEAPPC_WITH_MEDIA`); without it, per HANDOFF.md,
a click on the embed should open the file externally instead of playing
inline - which of the two happened is worth noting in the report.
